Research · Publication

ACM UMAP ADJUNCT '25 · ACM DIGITAL LIBRARY · 2025

JARVIS: Adaptive Dual-Hemisphere Architectures for Personalized Large Agentic Models

Adjunct Proceedings of the 33rd ACM Conference on User Modeling, Adaptation and Personalization

JARVIS introduces a two-hemisphere architecture for Large Agentic Models (LAMs) inspired by the biological organization of the human brain. The subjective hemisphere dynamically models user preferences and iteratively optimizes agent behavior through LoRA fine-tuning, Direct Preference Optimization, human feedback and synthetic data generation — a process we call "digital dreams." The objective hemisphere handles task execution through a graph of specialized agents. Together, they enable persistent, adaptive personalization at inference time.
